Lettering & markings

The only text on the aircraft is what you add here (stock model lettering removed). Click Place then click the model; drag a mark across the surface (including the belly: choose "Bottom" or place it on the underside); mouse-wheel over a mark resizes it (live inches), Shift+wheel rotates. Heights are real-world inches (model is 1:1, 19.76 m / 64 ft 10 in overall).

FAA quick-start

14 CFR 45.29(b)(3): rotorcraft nationality and registration marks must be at least 12 in high. 45.29(c): characters two-thirds as wide as high (“1” one-sixth; M and W may be as wide as high); (d) solid lines one-sixth as thick as the height; (e) spacing at least one-fourth of the character width. 45.27(a): displayed horizontally on both surfaces of the cabin, fuselage, boom or tail. 45.23(b): on limited, restricted, experimental or provisionally certificated aircraft, the applicable word near each entrance to the cabin, cockpit or pilot station, in letters not less than 2 in nor more than 6 in high. Source: eCFR 14 CFR part 45 (current to Oct 22, 2026; last amended 90 FR 35211, Jul 24, 2025). “FAA proportions” sets glyph widths and spacing per 45.29(c)/(e); stroke thickness follows the font. Check your own aircraft's certification basis.
